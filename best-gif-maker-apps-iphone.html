Quick answer

Choose by editing workflow, not by the longest feature list.

GIFmaker: GIF Maker & Editor is the focused option for turning photos, videos, and Live Photos into GIFs with per-frame timing, captions, filmstrip editing, reverse, and boomerang controls. Its listing says personal photos are processed on-device without an account. Optional iCloud history sync and web search are separate online features.

ImgPlay is the broad-format option, adding burst shots, screen recordings, frame-level editing, AI background removal, and export to GIF, MP4, APNG, or WebP. GIF Maker - Memes & GIFs is the AI-heavy option for text-generated GIFs, face swaps, memes, filters, and social effects.

Best focused private workflow

GIFmaker, when on-device editing, no account, detailed timing, and a simple GIF export flow matter most.

Best for broad formats

ImgPlay, when you need many source types, visual adjustments, stickers, and several export formats.

Best for AI and memes

GIF Maker - Memes & GIFs, when text generation, face swaps, meme tools, and social effects are the priority.

Check the result

Inspect the saved file before choosing a workflow.

Use a short sample you are comfortable importing. These are checks to perform yourself, not benchmark results or claims that we tested every paid feature.

File format and animation

Open the exported file separately from the editor preview. Check its extension and watch more than one loop. A static thumbnail alone does not prove the GIF is broken.

Size, readability and watermark

Check file size, image dimensions, small captions, frame changes and any watermark. If the output is too large for your destination, try a shorter clip, smaller dimensions or fewer frames, then inspect the new result.

Destination and paid limits

Send the sample through the app where you plan to use it and check the received copy. Verify the required export settings are included in the plan shown before confirming a trial or purchase.

Choose by workflow

The best app depends on what you are making.

A reaction loop, product demo, meme, and social video do not need the same editor. For a short video-to-GIF conversion, prioritize trimming, frame timing, loop direction, and export size. For a designed social asset, broader stickers, filters, fonts, and alternate formats may matter more.

AI tools are useful only when they match the job. Text generation and face swaps can create a starting point, but they do not replace precise timing or a clean first-to-last-frame transition in a traditional GIF.

Video or Live Photo

Choose an editor with trimming, frame-rate control, timing, and a preview that makes the restart point easy to inspect.

Meme or social post

Prioritize text tools, stickers, canvas ratios, platform exports, and any watermark or subscription limits that affect sharing.

Sensitive source media

Prefer an explicit on-device workflow, read the current privacy label, and avoid uploading private clips to features you do not need.

Cost and compatibility

Compare the upfront price and optional paid plans.

The US App Store lists GIFmaker at $0.99 upfront as checked on October 29, 2026. ImgPlay and GIF Maker - Memes & GIFs are free to download. ImgPlay describes Premium plans, including watermark removal and advanced saving options. GIF Maker lists subscription tiers for unlimited creation and premium AI, filters, stickers, and fonts. GIFmaker's current description presents its core editor without advertising a subscription.

Check the App Store purchase sheet immediately before confirming. Trial length, renewal interval, regional price, and included features can change independently of this page.

Check the export before subscribing

Confirm resolution, watermark behavior, file format, frame limits, and whether the free result works in the destination app.

Check device support

GIFmaker currently requires iOS 17.0, while the two competitors in this comparison currently list iOS 15.0.

Cancel from Apple settings

For any recurring plan, review the renewal date and manage the subscription in your Apple Account settings.

FAQ

GIF maker app questions answered.

What is the best GIF maker app for iPhone?

Choose by task: GIFmaker for on-device personal-media editing and frame timing, ImgPlay for multiple import and export formats, or GIF Maker for AI generation and meme effects. GIFmaker also offers optional iCloud history sync and web search. Compare the paid-feature limits and check a saved export before choosing.

Which iPhone apps can turn a video into a GIF?

All three current App Store listings say they can turn videos into GIFs. GIFmaker adds per-frame timing and filmstrip controls, ImgPlay adds extensive frame editing and multiple export formats, and GIF Maker combines conversion with AI and meme tools.

Are these GIF maker apps free?

As checked on October 29, 2026, GIFmaker costs $0.99 upfront in the US App Store. ImgPlay and GIF Maker are free to download with paid features. ImgPlay lists watermark removal and advanced saving under Premium; GIF Maker lists unlimited video and photo GIF creation among Premium features. Check your local purchase sheet for current terms.

Which GIF maker works without an account or media uploads?

GIFmaker says personal-photo editing is processed on-device without an account. Its optional iCloud history sync and web search are separate online features, so the whole app should not be described as having no network activity. Review each app's current privacy label and the particular feature you plan to use; App Store privacy disclosures are developer-provided.

Do I need AI features to make a GIF on iPhone?

No. Video trimming, frame order, timing, captions, canvas controls, and loop direction are enough for most GIFs. AI features are optional when you specifically want text-to-GIF generation, face swaps, or automatic background removal.
