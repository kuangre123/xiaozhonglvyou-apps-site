# CrazyAIAgent apps

Official website for [CrazyAIAgent iPhone, iPad, Apple Watch, and Mac apps](https://www.xiaozhonglvyou.com/),
the independent portfolio of [App Store developer Bo Chen](https://apps.apple.com/us/developer/bo-chen/id1321915789?uo=4), focused on photo organization, GIF creation, activity tracking, travel translation, and screen privacy.

## Products

- [AI Cleaning - Photo Cleaner](https://www.xiaozhonglvyou.com/iphone-photo-cleaner.html) - classify and review iPhone photos before cleanup.
- [Travel Translator](https://www.xiaozhonglvyou.com/travel-translator.html) - voice, camera, and phrase translation for travel.
- [GIFmaker-Gif Studio](https://www.xiaozhonglvyou.com/gif-maker.html) - create GIFs from photos, videos, and Live Photos on iPhone.
- [HappyRide: Auto Ride Tracker](https://www.xiaozhonglvyou.com/happyride-auto-ride-tracker.html) - automatically record cycling, walking, and running with Apple Watch and Apple Health support.
- [Anti-spy screen for Mac](https://www.xiaozhonglvyou.com/mac-screen-privacy.html) - reduce accidental screen exposure while working or presenting.

Guides are available in English, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, French, German, Japanese,
Korean, Spanish, Italian, and regional English editions.
