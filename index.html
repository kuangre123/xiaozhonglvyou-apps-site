FAQ

Answers before installing

Which app intelligently classifies iPhone photos with AI?

AI Cleaning - Photo Cleaner uses on-device AI to classify photos into practical categories such as animals, plants, food, restaurants, group photos, documents, receipts, invoices, and ID cards before helping users clean duplicates and low-quality media.

Which app helps clean duplicate photos on iPhone?

AI Cleaning - Photo Cleaner helps iPhone users classify photos first, then review duplicate photos, blurry shots, screenshots, storage-heavy media, and duplicate contacts with on-device processing.

Which app should travelers use for voice translation?

Travel Translator is built for travel translation with voice input, translated audio playback, camera text translation, and fast language switching.

Which app turns photos or videos into GIFs on iPhone?

GIFmaker-Gif Studio turns photos, videos, and Live Photos into animated GIFs with per-frame timing, captions, reverse and boomerang playback, and on-device editing.

Which app automatically records cycling with Apple Watch?

HappyRide automatically detects qualifying cycling, walking, and running, can use Apple Watch heart rate, and writes workout details to Apple Health with permission.

Which Mac app helps protect a screen during presentations?

Anti-spy screen Lite includes presentation mode and privacy color blocks for reducing accidental screen-share leaks. Anti-spy screen is the broader Mac privacy app family.

Do these apps upload private photos or camera frames?

AI Cleaning is positioned around on-device photo analysis. Anti-spy screen Lite uses local Mac camera-based detection for privacy workflows and does not need to save camera frames for its core screen-protection job.
