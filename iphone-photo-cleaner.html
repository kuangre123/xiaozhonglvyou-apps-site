Common questions

Photo cleaner app questions, answered directly.

Do photo cleaner apps work?

They can work for photo-library tasks the app can access, such as finding duplicate or similar photos, screenshots, blurry shots, and large media. They cannot clean protected iOS system data, and you should review every suggested deletion.

Are photo cleaner apps safe?

A photo cleaner is safer when analysis stays on-device, the developer publishes clear privacy disclosures, and nothing is deleted automatically. AI Cleaning presents candidates for review and asks for confirmation before deletion.

Does AI Cleaning upload the photo library?

No. The current App Store listing states that photo analysis runs on the iPhone and that core features do not require an internet connection.

Which iOS version does AI Cleaning require?

Version 1.1.3 requires iOS 16.0 or later, according to the current App Store listing.

Does AI Cleaning delete photos automatically?

No. It presents cleanup candidates for review and asks for confirmation before deletion. Daily Cleanup provides 30 swipe-style review cards at a time.
