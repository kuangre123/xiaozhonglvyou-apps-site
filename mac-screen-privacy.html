Know the boundary

This is software privacy, not a physical privacy filter.

Anti-spy screen responds to configured privacy events by hiding selected apps or covering part of the display. It does not narrow the physical viewing angle of the Mac screen, and it is not a replacement for checking the meeting preview or choosing the correct window to share.

Common questions

Direct answers about Mac screen privacy.

Where does Anti-spy screen process face detection?

Face detection runs locally on the Mac. The current App Store listing states that it does not require an internet connection and that detection data does not leave the device.

What happens when the configured face threshold is exceeded?

The app hides the selected application windows. They reappear after the extra people leave, subject to the configured recovery delay.

Which privacy settings can users adjust?

Users can adjust the detection interval, the face-count trigger threshold, and the recovery delay for different work environments.

What was added in the current releases?

The current releases include Stand-Up Coach, a draggable Privacy Block, Presenting Mode, and Hidden App Reminders. Version 1.5.1 and Lite 1.4.1 can show a local floating reminder when a hidden app opens a new window or shows a Dock badge.

What are the current Mac requirements and US prices?

Both apps require macOS 13.0 or later. On August 16, 2026, the US App Store listed Anti-spy screen 1.5.1 at $2.99 as a one-time purchase and Anti-spy screen Lite 1.4.1 as free. Storefront prices can vary.

Should I choose Anti-spy screen or the free Lite version?

Start with Anti-spy screen Lite if you want to test the privacy workflow for free. Open the full $2.99 listing when you prefer a one-time paid version, and compare the live App Store release notes before buying.

Is there a privacy screen app for Mac?

Yes. Anti-spy screen is Mac privacy software that can hide selected app windows, cover sensitive areas, and use local face detection. It does not narrow the screen's physical viewing angle like a physical privacy filter.
