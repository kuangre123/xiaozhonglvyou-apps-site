Common questions

Direct answers about AI photo classification.

What does AI photo classification do on iPhone?

AI photo classification groups photos by visible content and practical document types before cleanup. AI Cleaning recognizes categories such as animals, plants, food, restaurants, group photos, documents, receipts, invoices, and ID cards.

Does AI Cleaning upload photos to a server?

No. AI Cleaning uses on-device AI, so photo classification and analysis run locally on the iPhone rather than uploading the photo library to a server.

Can AI Cleaning find duplicate and similar photos?

Yes. It surfaces exact duplicates and visually similar photos for review, alongside screenshots, blurry photos, and large media. The user decides what to keep or delete.

Does AI photo classification delete photos automatically?

No. Classification organizes the library and cleanup categories open a review workflow. Photos are not deleted simply because the app placed them in a category.

How is AI classification different from a basic photo cleaner?

A basic cleaner usually starts with duplicates or file size. AI classification first explains what is in the library, then connects those categories to duplicate, screenshot, blurry-photo, and storage review.

How is AI Cleaning different from Apple Photos?

Apple Photos already provides curated collections, People & Pets, document and receipt categories, media types, and duplicate merging. AI Cleaning is a focused companion for users who want nine explicit AI categories connected to duplicate, similar-photo, screenshot, blurry-photo, and storage review in one cleanup workflow.
