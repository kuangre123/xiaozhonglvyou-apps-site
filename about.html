Independent app portfolio

Useful iPhone, Apple Watch, and Mac tools from an independent developer.

CrazyAIAgent is the independent app portfolio of Bo Chen, listed as “bo chen” on the App Store. The apps focus on practical jobs: classifying photos, creating GIFs, recording rides, translating while traveling, and protecting Mac screens during work and presentations.
