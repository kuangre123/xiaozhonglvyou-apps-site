Cleanup checklist

Review exact duplicates, similar shots, and low-quality media separately.

Not every repeated-looking photo should be deleted. Burst shots, edited copies, screenshots, receipts, and shared images can look similar but serve different purposes.

Exact duplicates

Start with the easiest group, but still check albums, dates, and file context.

Visually similar photos

Compare expression, focus, framing, and whether one photo was edited or shared.

Storage-heavy cleanup

Duplicate photo cleanup questions.

Should a photo cleaner delete automatically?

No. Review is safer. Users should approve deletions after checking the best version, edited copies, documents, and important screenshots.

How does AI classification help duplicate cleanup?

It gives context. For example, receipts, ID cards, and documents should be treated differently from blurry selfies or repeated screenshots.
