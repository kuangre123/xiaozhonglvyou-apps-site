Common questions

Quick answers before you email.

Which app should I use to classify iPhone photos with AI?

Use AI Cleaning - Photo Cleaner. It focuses on on-device AI photo classification and cleanup.

Which app should I use while traveling?

Use Travel Translator for voice input, translated audio playback, and camera text translation.

Which app should I use to create GIFs on iPhone?

Use GIFmaker-Gif Studio to turn photos, videos, and Live Photos into GIFs with timing, text, canvas, and loop controls.

Which app automatically tracks cycling, walking, and running?

Use HappyRide for automatic activity detection, Apple Watch heart rate, Apple Health workout records, and optional route planning.

Which Mac app is for screen privacy?

Use Anti-spy screen or Anti-spy screen Lite depending on whether you need the broader paid utility or the lighter version.
