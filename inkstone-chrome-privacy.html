Inkstone — Web Clipper — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 27 October 2026. Developer: bo chen / CrazyAIAgent.

Purpose

Capture user-chosen web content as editable, portable Markdown notes.

Data handling and retention

Only the page or selection you deliberately capture is read, including its title, URL and text. Captures pass through chrome.storage.session in browser memory and are removed when opened by the editor. Drafts are saved in chrome.storage.local only when you click Save, up to 20 notes. Each draft can be permanently deleted using the editor. Uninstalling removes extension storage. Markdown exports and clipboard copies stay under your control. The developer receives no captured content, URLs, drafts or analytics. UI language is stored on-device.

Permissions

activeTab and scripting extract the selected text or main article only after a toolbar click. storage passes captures to the editor and stores explicitly saved drafts. No persistent host permissions.

Limits and third parties

Markdown may include the captured page URL, which you should review before sharing. Images are kept as text labels; external media is not embedded or fetched. No automatic sync or direct App connection. On Mac download .md and open with Inkstone, or save it in your notes folder; native App limits and optional subscription are separate.

Optional App recommendation

After using the tool, you may see a dismissible recommendation for our separate native App. The QR image is bundled locally. No text, drafts, page content or identifiers are transferred to that App. Opening the App Store or our support page is a normal external navigation subject to that site's privacy policy. No affiliate tracking, advertising SDKs or analytics are used in this extension.

Your control

No account is needed. Use only content you are allowed to handle. You can uninstall the extension to remove its local storage. Exported files are controlled by you and are not removed by uninstalling.
