PrivacyGuard — Web Redactor — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 27 October 2026. Developer: bo chen / CrazyAIAgent.

Purpose

Visual masking of user-chosen webpage text for presentations.

Data handling and retention

Page text, including emails or currency values you choose to mask, is processed in the active page only. Original text is retained in page memory to support restoration, then released on reload. No page text, names, emails, financial data or masks are sent to the developer or stored as a browsing history. UI language is stored on-device.

Permissions

activeTab and scripting allow access only after a toolbar action, to inject local masking code into the main frame. There are no persistent host permissions, camera permission, native messaging or access to other applications.

Limits and third parties

Visual masking is not irreversible redaction or access control. Original data remains in page memory. A website can modify or reveal content after masking. Inspect every screen before sharing. Frames, images, browser UI, PDFs and other applications are not covered.

Optional App recommendation

After using the tool, you may see a dismissible recommendation for our separate native App. The QR image is bundled locally. No text, drafts, page content or identifiers are transferred to that App. Opening the App Store or our support page is a normal external navigation subject to that site's privacy policy. No affiliate tracking, advertising SDKs or analytics are used in this extension.

Your control

No account is needed. Use only content you are allowed to handle. You can uninstall the extension to remove its local storage. Exported files are controlled by you and are not removed by uninstalling.
