Translation Specialist — Web Text — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 27 October 2026. Developer: bo chen / CrazyAIAgent.

Purpose

Translate text selected by the user or pasted into the editor.

Data handling and retention

Selected text passes through chrome.storage.session in browser memory and is removed when loaded into the editor. Original and translated text are not saved as a history. Chrome on-device translation processes text locally using browser-managed language packs; Chrome may contact Google to download the required models. The developer receives no text or analytics. UI language is stored on-device.

Permissions

activeTab and scripting read the selected text only after the toolbar is clicked. contextMenus adds an explicit selection-translation action. storage passes a selection to its new editor tab using temporary browser-session memory. No persistent host permissions, screen capture, microphone or camera access.

Limits and third parties

Online translation is optional. Clicking Send text to Google Translate sends your text and language pair to https://translate.google.com/ as URL query parameters in a new tab. Google receives that data; its privacy policy applies (https://policies.google.com/privacy). The resulting URL may be retained in ordinary browser history or synced according to your browser settings. Avoid confidential text. There is no automatic online fallback. Online mode is limited to 1,500 characters; local input is limited to 5,000. The extension handles text, not screenshot OCR, voice or camera translation. Those are separate iPhone App features.

Optional App recommendation

After using the tool, you may see a dismissible recommendation for our separate native App. The QR image is bundled locally. No text, drafts, page content or identifiers are transferred to that App. Opening the App Store or our support page is a normal external navigation subject to that site's privacy policy. No affiliate tracking, advertising SDKs or analytics are used in this extension.

Your control

No account is needed. Use only content you are allowed to handle. You can uninstall the extension to remove its local storage. Exported files are controlled by you and are not removed by uninstalling.
